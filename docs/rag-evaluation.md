# RAG Retrieval Evaluation

Generated by `RagEvaluationTest` (`RAG_EVAL_REPORT=true`). Dataset: 4 fictional resumes, 28 labelled queries with at least one relevant section, 6 queries with no relevant section (`backend/src/test/resources/rag-eval/`). Retrieval is exact cosine search within one resume, the same scope as production. Embedding: `local-hash-v1` (local feature hashing, lexical).

## Experiment 1: chunking strategy

### 1a. Same context budget (800 characters per requirement)

Chunks are taken in rank order until the budget is used (at least one chunk). This is the fair comparison: the real constraint is how much evidence fits in the prompt.

| Strategy | Chunks / resume | Recall | Context precision | Chars retrieved / query |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| structured | 7.8 | 89.3% | 31.7% | 668.7 |
| fixed-300/50 | 5.3 | 85.7% | 33.2% | 588.4 |
| fixed-800/150 | 2.3 | 82.1% | 27.8% | 698.9 |

### 1b. Same k (k = 3) — misleading on its own

With only ~2 chunks per resume, fixed-800 retrieves almost the whole resume at k = 3, so its recall is inflated.

| Strategy | Recall@3 | Precision@3 | MRR |
|---|---|---|---|
| structured | 89.3% | 31.0% | 0.72 |
| fixed-300/50 | 92.9% | 38.1% | 0.83 |
| fixed-800/150 | 100.0% | 53.0% | 0.91 |
